## Quick Recommendations

- **Small servers (1-10 players):** View 8, Simulation 6
- **Medium servers (10-30 players):** View 6-8, Simulation 4-6
- **Large servers (30+ players):** View 4-6, Simulation 3-4

## What These Settings Do

- **view-distance** controls how many chunks are sent to players
- **simulation-distance** controls how many chunks are ticked (mob AI, redstone, farms)

Simulation distance has the biggest impact on CPU usage.

## Where to Change Them

Edit `server.properties`:

```properties
view-distance=6
simulation-distance=4
```

Or use our [server.properties generator](/guides/server-properties-generator).

## How to Pick the Right Values

### 1. Start Conservative

Lower values reduce CPU and memory usage. If your TPS stays at 20, you can raise them gradually.

### 2. Match Your Hardware

If your CPU is the bottleneck, lower **simulation-distance** first. If your network is the bottleneck, lower **view-distance**.

### 3. Consider Gameplay Style

Redstone-heavy servers need higher simulation distance. Survival servers can often run with 4-6.
